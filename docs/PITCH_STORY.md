# Pitch story — 90 seconds

**Problem.** Renewable generation can be available but restricted by system conditions. Looking only backward tells us what was lost, not what to do next.

**Step 1 — Understand.** Our analytics layer identifies when, where and under which system conditions curtailment concentrates.

**Step 2 — Anticipate.** A time-aware model estimates the probability of curtailment and the expected energy at risk. The interface also shows the strongest local drivers of the forecast.

**Step 3 — Act.** If the risk is critical, the platform does not claim it can override the system operator. It asks a defensible question: under the mitigation resources actually available in this scenario, how much of the energy could be absorbed instead of lost?

**Step 4 — Prove impact.** The optimizer compares recovered and remaining MWh and converts them into transparent scenario KPIs.

**Message.** We are not building only a predictor. We are building a decision-support loop from anticipation to measurable mitigation.
